# Credits

The Star Control total conversion is a fan project. It is not affiliated
with or endorsed by the owners of Star Control.

## Star Control

Star Control and Star Control II were created by Fred Ford and Paul Reiche
III (Toys for Bob), published by Accolade in 1990 and 1992. The ships,
races, weapons and setting of this add-on come from those games.

## Art and sounds: The Ur-Quan Masters

The ship sprites, projectiles, explosions, hit effects and sound effects are
converted from the content of **The Ur-Quan Masters**, the open-source
version of Star Control II, by `tools/make_art.py`. They come from the
[UQM MegaMod content](https://github.com/JHGuitarFreak/UQM-MegaMod-Content)
by Serosis (JHGuitarFreak) and contributors:

- original Star Control II art and sounds (`base/`), released by Toys for Bob
  through The Ur-Quan Masters project
- the MegaMod's HD content by Kohr-Ah Death (`addons/mm-hd/`)

These files are under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/2.5/)
licence (CC BY-NC-SA 2.5), and so are the add-on's files made from them.
Every one of these files is listed in `art-manifest.txt`. This add-on is free
and must stay non-commercial.
